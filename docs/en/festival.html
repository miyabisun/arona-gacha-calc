5.5th Anniversary Festival Comparison

miyabisun/arona-gacha-calc

Festival recruitment doubles the 3★ rate to 6%, and festival students you did not select can also appear. Because such an off-target pull can still hand you another student you wanted, the 200-pull milestones of Recruitment Points become competitive.

Assumptions
  • The 3★ rate during festival recruitment is 6.00%.
  • The student you select appears at 0.70%. Under Recruitment Charge, that becomes 50% at charge 99 and 100% at charge 199.
  • The 9 remaining festival students — 10 in total, minus the one you selected — share 0.90% equally, giving 0.10% each.
  • The remaining 4.40% covers permanent 3★ students. It is recorded for completeness but never used in the calculation.
  • The first-time pickup bonus is granted only by pulling the student you selected or exchanging Recruitment Points. An off-target pull never grants it.
  • The strategy always selects a student you do not own yet; once every student is owned, it selects an owned one to collect the remaining bonuses.
  • Eleph and shards are counted separately. Most players sit on a large shard stock, so shards cannot carry the same weight as Eleph.

But stop the moment she arrives

Carrying on to 99 pulls after the hit drops the efficiency of those extra pulls to about 1.1 Eleph each. Stop when she lands and the outlay settles at exactly 45.1 pulls — the same efficiency as chasing her outright.

Who this suits

  • Worth it if you still want Eleph on the selected student. The Pyroxene efficiency matches chasing her outright, with festival off-target pulls added on top. Even a miss returns an average of 36.6 pulls on the next limited banner.
  • Not worth it if there is nowhere left to spend Eleph. You are buying shards and 3★ students you will never build. The line is simply whether one student's worth of Eleph still has a use.

Recruitment Points lets you choose your upside

To be clear up front, if you are only after two students, the two systems land in much the same place. Recruitment Points all but guarantees you spend a full 200 pulls, but in return 13.7% of the time the exchange is left entirely spare. A spare exchange is 100 Eleph, or 200 if you send it into a Uniform Nel you already own. That is the whole of the difference.

The gap only opens up once you are after three or more. Recruitment Points lets you stay on Swimsuit Iroha the whole way while directing each 200-point exchange where it helps most. Recruitment Charge offers no such choice.

That said, letting a festival student slip away is never an option. If Ibuki is not owned, the first exchange always goes to securing her. The new system reaches that same point reliably too, so nothing separates them here.

The difference shows up in the second exchange. What it is worth depends on who you spend it on: a student you do not own yields the unit plus a 100 Eleph first-time bonus, while one you already own whose bonus is still unspent — say a player sitting on Uniform Nel at UE3 — pays 100 Eleph for the duplicate plus the 100 Eleph bonus, for 200 Eleph.

A single pull is worth more during the festival

Pyroxene has been counted only as "how many pulls it buys". But what a pull returns also differs by period: festival recruitment doubles the 3★ rate to 6%, so the same ten pulls leave more shards behind.

This gap accrues from the pulls alone, with no bearing on selecting or exchanging. But shards picked up are not the same as combat power. Most students never enter a build at all, and the ones that do were taken to UE2 through the shard shop long ago. At best a tenth of what you collect finds a use, and by then the cheap exchange tiers are spent, so it costs five shards per Eleph.

Discounted that way, the 600 shard gap per 200 pulls is really worth about 12 Eleph. Pulling during the festival is still the better deal, but not by enough to change which system comes out ahead.

Both the shard counts and the one-tenth usable share are estimates from play. Pinning down the 2★ and 1★ breakdown will move the numbers, but not the conclusion: the nominal shard total overstates what you actually gain.

Where the run actually ends

Everything so far assumed a full 400 pulls. In practice you stop at the end of the block where the last student arrives. Nobody spends another 200 pulls on a finished roster just to farm Eleph. Each exchange still goes to a student you do not own, whenever one remains.
