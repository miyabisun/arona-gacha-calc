5.5th Anniversary Festival Comparison

miyabisun/arona-gacha-calc

Festival recruitment doubles the 3★ rate to 6%, and festival students you did not select can also appear. Because such an off-target pull can still hand you another student you wanted, the 200-pull milestones of Recruitment Points become competitive.

Assumptions

  • The 3★ rate during festival recruitment is 6.00%.
  • The student you select appears at 0.70%. Under Recruitment Charge, that becomes 50% at charge 99 and 100% at charge 199.
  • The 9 remaining festival students — 10 in total, minus the one you selected — share 0.90% equally, giving 0.10% each.
  • The remaining 4.40% covers permanent 3★ students. It is recorded for completeness but never used in the calculation.
  • The first-time pickup bonus is granted only by pulling the student you selected or exchanging Recruitment Points. An off-target pull never grants it.
  • The strategy always selects a student you do not own yet; once every student is owned, it selects an owned one to collect the remaining bonuses.

Playing off-target pulls as upside on Recruitment Points

Of the four featured students, the attackers do not pull their weight until UE2. That raises the question of whether it works to keep selecting one priority student and leave the rest to exchanges and off-target pulls. The comparison is the ordinary approach of filling in whoever you do not own yet.

  • The priority is a single attacker such as Swimsuit Iroha or Uniform Nel, aiming for UE2 (340 Eleph).
  • Each hit on the priority pays 100 Eleph, and every hit after the first adds 50 shards, converted at the cheapest available rate. Three hits reach 353 Eleph — enough for UE2.
  • For the other three the goal is simply owning them, which Recruitment Points guarantees through its exchange every 200 pulls.
  • Two starting points are shown: owning none of them, and already owning one (Nel or Rio picked up earlier).

Under Recruitment Points, staying on the priority does not strand the other three — the exchange fills them in regardless. Starting with one already owned, 400 pulls completes the other three while still reaching UE2 more often than spreading out. In that case focusing costs you almost nothing.

Doing the same under Recruitment Charge gives the highest UE2 rate of all, but with no exchange the other three depend entirely on off-target pulls, landing only about one of them even by 400 pulls. Only Recruitment Points lets you focus and still collect the rest, which is the one arrangement where off-target pulls work as genuine upside.

Is chasing off-target pulls a strategy?

If an off-target pull lands before you hit the student you selected, exchanging for that same student pays 100 Eleph for the duplicate, 100 more from the unspent first-time bonus, and 50 shards all at once. To judge whether that is worth chasing, here is the chance an off-target pull arrives before your first hit.

Recruitment Points wins here because its first hit takes 142.9 pulls on average, keeping the slots alive that whole time. Recruitment Charge settles in 90.1 pulls, with the 50% slot at pull 100 and the guarantee at pull 200 cutting the wait short.

Chasing it deliberately is still not worth it. Prioritising an off-target student for the exchange adds only 3 Eleph over 400 pulls (504 becomes 507 when targeting three), while the chance of owning everyone drops 2.7pt. Going further and holding a student you already own just to keep the slots open gains 36 Eleph but costs 29.7pt of completion. Selecting is a 0.7% chance; each waiting slot is 0.1%. Trading away a method 3.5 times stronger to preserve those slots never pays off.

Carrying 99 pulls into the next limited banner

Recruitment Charge carries over within a banner category. Charge built up on a festival banner carries into the next limited or festival banner, while charge from the standard banner carries into the next standard banner.

That makes a plan possible: stop at 99 pulls during the festival banner, then open the next limited banner already at charge 99. The 50% guaranteed slot lands on your very first pull, and even if it misses, the charge-199 guarantee arrives on the 101st pull.

Where to build the charge

The same 99 pulls yield different by-products depending on where you spend them. A festival banner runs 3★ at 6.00%, so 99 pulls return 5.94 of them on average, while a limited banner stays at 3.00% and returns only 2.97. Building the charge during a festival banner collects roughly twice as much for the same number of pulls.

A duplicate permanent 3★ is worth 30 Eleph and 50 shards, so how much that matters depends on how many students you actually want inside the permanent pool. Twice the pulls does not mean twice the value.

Pulling the student you selected resets the charge to 0, so the plan only survives half the time: the chance of still holding the charge after 99 pulls is 49.89%. Banking all the way to 199 to finish the next banner in a single pull fails even more often — about 87.5% lose the charge before reaching it.
